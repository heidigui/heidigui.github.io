Why do we care about astroparticle physics?

GOOD QUESTION!!! ✧٩(•́⌄•́๑)و ✧

A scientific anwser would be: We can further understand the cosmos!
╔═.✵.═══════════════════════════════════════════╗
✧ In 1991, a UHE cosmic ray dubbed the Oh-My-God particle was discovered. The engery of this particle was unheard of, but determined to likely be from an extragalactic source. As of today, we still don't know it's origin!
✧ Since then, astrophyscisists have developed UHE detectors to find more of these rare energetic events. If we can detect a signal, we can use measurments to determine where it came from, and probe what lies outside the Milky Way! ╚═══════════════════════════════════════════.✵.═╝

A general answer would be: Also that we can further understand the cosmos! But maybe for a different reason..
╔═.✵.═══════════════════════════════════════════╗
✧ One of the biggest questions in science is: How was the Universe created? To answer this mind boggling problem, we must first understand the space around us! The solar system was the first step, but whats next? We must explore whats further out there.. and astroparticle physics is one of our strongest tools we can use!
✧ Additionally, humanity has existed for a miniscule moment in the history of Earth, let alone the Universe! We have always searched to understand what came before us, whether that is through studying history, achaeloegy, geology, etc..., it is intrinsic in our human nature to learn more◝(ᵔᗜᵔ)◜! ╚═══════════════════════════════════════════.✵.═╝
